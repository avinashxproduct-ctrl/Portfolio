01 · Overview

Fleet Dashboard

Fleet Dashboard is a centralized fleet management tool built for a leading international shipping and container transportation company. The company's roots stretch back to the 19th century, when its founding companies put their first ships to sea carrying general cargo and passengers to New York.

My roleAs the UX/UI Designer, I designed clear, easy-to-use interfaces and visuals that help make better decisions, by focusing on what end users actually need.

02 · Problem identification

Fragmented data, slow decisions

Fleet Dashboard needed a centralized dashboard to streamline fleet management by consolidating fragmented data. They sought real-time insights, KPI tracking, and support for decision-making to enhance operations and meet sustainability goals.

The goal: an integrated dashboard with…

Like fuel consumption and average weather, tracked over time and comparable across the fleet.
Vessel, voyage and port-pair information in one place instead of scattered across systems.
Side-by-side views that support decisions and sustainability goals.
