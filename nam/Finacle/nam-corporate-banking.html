Overview

Designed a white-label corporate banking platform tailored for North America, addressing challenges like fragmented workflows, dense forms, and poor user guidance. Through research, wireframing, UI, and prototyping, I helped create a scalable solution that streamlined features, reduced friction, and improved task completion for both SMEs and large enterprises.
