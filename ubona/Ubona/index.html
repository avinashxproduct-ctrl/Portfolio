What brought them to us?

  • Ubona approached us as they were diversifying their business by providing IVR services to banks & brokerage firms, including for IIFL Securities
  • They aimed to introduce an IVR-based mobile experience for IIFL Securities and sought our expertise to design a seamless mobile product for Demat and Trading onboarding, ensuring an intuitive user journey integrated with their voice-driven technology

What is a Demat & Trading Account?

  • Demat means Dematerialized account which holds financial Securities in an electronic form
  • Trading account is used to execute, buy and sell orders. Buying credits shares to your linked Demat account & Conversely debits shares when you sell them.

How a Demat Account is created?

  1. IIFL
    Choose a Depository Participant
  2. Fill out the form & provide required documents
  3. KYC Compliance
  4. Sign the Agreement
  5. Receive Demat Account Number

Who all are involved in this?

Investor

An investor allocates capital into various assets to generate returns and grow wealth over time

Depository Participant

It is an intermediary that helps investors open and manage demat accounts for holding and trading securities electronically

Depository – NSDL/CDSL

A central organization that holds securities in electronic form. The DP is connected to a depository to hold the securities

SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India)

SEBI regulates DPs and brokerage firms to safeguard investors’ interests and ensure compliance with financial laws.

KYC Agencies

KYC agencies verify an applicant’s identity and address during Demat account creation to ensure compliance and prevent fraud.

Banks

If you need to fund your trading account, a payment bank may facilitate the transfer of money to your trading account.

Why are we doing this?

Filling out the application without any support was confusing and frustrating for me😣
I often felt lost during the process because there wasn’t anyone to help me through the steps😩
Opening a Demat account feels like it takes forever because of all the paperwork😤

1L+users

With more than 1L+ users creating a Demat Account per day, enhancing this experience for the users was crucial.

We identified a few problems with the traditional account creation flow:

  • Traditional Demat account opening involves lengthy paperwork, in-person verification, making it time-consuming and prone to errors.
  • Many users struggle with the absence of real-time assistance, leading to frustration, incomplete applications or delays
The challenge

How might we guide first-time users through Demat account opening with voice plus visual guidance, so they feel supported, complete the flow, and don’t drop off or call support?

How did we approach it?

Desk research

Understood what a Demat account and trading are, how an account is created, and who is involved in the process.

Input from Ubona

Worked with the Ubona team to understand their Visual IVR technology and what it needed to support.

Review & refine

The design was reviewed along the way and fine-tuned from feedback by the Ubona team and the internal design team.

How are we solving this problem?

  • By leveraging Visual IVR technology:
    Visual IVR (Interactive Voice Response) is a technology that helps customers interact with a company’s services using both voice and a visual interface on their smartphones or computers.

How does Visual IVR work?

  1. 1

    User calls the Customer care

    Customer agent sends them a link via SMS through which users can perform the task with the help of Smart or Visual IVR

  2. 2

    User initiates the Demat Account Creation

  3. 3

    User is welcomed by the IVR Assistant

    that provides both audio and visual instructions to facilitate the account creation process

  4. 4

    User completes KYC Details with Guidance

  5. 5

    User submits the Account Application

    IIFL will further verify the details for approval
